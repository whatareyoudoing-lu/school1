📜 How to Play
1. Secret Poison Priming: Player 1 secretly clicks 1 card to be their poison (Player 2 looks away). Next, Player 2 secretly clicks 1 card to be their poison (Player 1 looks away).
2. Duel & Meaning Challenge: Take turns clicking a card. Before clicking to reveal, say its English meaning out loud based on the Chinese & Pinyin on the front!
3. Glass Shatter & Game Over: If anyone flips either poison card, a bottle shatter sound plays, the screen shakes, and that player gets poisoned and loses immediately!
